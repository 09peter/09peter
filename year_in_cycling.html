25–30 bpm puts you at "fit recreational." 30+ is "well-trained." Stable across the year — neither winter nor crash dented it. Even better at 105 kg, where the heart is moving more weight against gravity.

The Surprise

Most amateurs fade after 90 minutes. I systematically negative-split — finishing stronger than I started. The data is unambiguous on this.
